The latest speed anchor is τ − 15 min, and every input must have capture_time_local ≤ τ. Targets are same-node provider snapshots at τ + H, with capture_time_local > τ. Ridge uses training-only scaling/fitting and validation-selected alpha values 100/10/10, without validation refitting. Points are descriptive estimates without confidence intervals. Horizons and layers differ in sample composition, and availability is not measurement accuracy or prediction correctness. The design uses a historical record-time proxy rather than verified online arrival, and provider references are not ground truth.
